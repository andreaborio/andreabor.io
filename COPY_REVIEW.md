# Copy review

The previous draft relied on a joke in almost every block: cloud bills, suffering GPUs, warehouse parties, personality traits, neighbours, and cries for help. Any one of those lines could work. Together they create the smooth, predictable rhythm readers now associate with generated copy.

This revision follows the stronger patterns in Andrea's own Substack posts:

- start from a test, number, or failure rather than a generic self-description;
- keep technical nouns that identify the actual work: Metal, SSD streaming, MoE, GLM-5.2, 64GB, and 1,328 tensors;
- use short sentences as turns in the argument, not as automatic punchlines;
- make fewer jokes and let the situation carry the humour;
- avoid repeated three-part lists, fake oppositions, motivational conclusions, and claims broader than the evidence;
- do not add deliberate mistakes or optimize for an AI detector.

The result is still edited copy. The aim is not to disguise its production history; it is to make every line specific enough to belong on Andrea's site.
