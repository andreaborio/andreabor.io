The useful part of an experiment usually happens after the screenshot.

A model runs once. The number looks good. Then the second run changes it, the cache turns out to be warm, or the comparison was not paired. The result becomes less exciting and more useful.

I wanted one place for that version of the work.

I build local LLM tooling, mostly around quantization, Apple Silicon, and streaming models that do not fit comfortably in memory. The posts here keep the commands, measurements, failures, and caveats close together. Some started on my Substack. New work will land here too.

This is not a content machine. I will publish when there is something I can explain and, preferably, reproduce.

That is the whole plan.
