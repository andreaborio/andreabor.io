I'm Andrea, a software developer working on local LLM inference.

I build [Hebrus](https://github.com/andreaborio/hebrus), a Metal-first inference engine for Apple Silicon with adaptive SSD streaming. Most of the work starts with a model that does not fit, a machine that should not be enough, and a measurement I do not trust until I can reproduce it.

Before this I worked on cloud infrastructure. Some habits survived: make the bottleneck visible, test the obvious answer, and keep the claim narrower than the evidence.

I also work on [forgequant](https://github.com/andreaborio/forgequant), [benchy](https://github.com/andreaborio/benchy), and an experimental [ds4 fork](https://github.com/andreaborio/ds4). Model artifacts and experimental builds live on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/andreaborio). This site holds the field notes: what ran, what stalled, and which promising idea got slower when measured properly.

## What this site is for

This is where I keep my longest-running experiments built with AI models. Keeping them here is a commitment: an experiment that lands on this site stays here, dated, and is not rewritten later to look better.

The reason is to look back. The same kind of project, written up at different times by different models, shows how the work changes from one model to the next. The posts are part of the evidence. Many of them are written by the model that did the work, from the commits and logs. The code and the reasoning behind it get evaluated without a formal benchmark: the record does that over time.

These projects are not autonomous. I steer them. I write the requirements and the specifications, I make the product and design decisions, I reject what does not work, and sometimes I prescribe how something must be implemented. The models write the code and most of the text. When a post describes a decision, that decision was mine or was approved by me. When it describes an implementation, the implementation was usually the model's.

Away from the terminal, I mix techno, UK garage, and whatever other underground neurospicy shit has made it into the folder that week.
